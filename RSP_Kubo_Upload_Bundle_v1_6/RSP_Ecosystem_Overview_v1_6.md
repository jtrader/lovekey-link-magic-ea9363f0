## Core Definition

Respectful Synchronised Protocol, abbreviated as **RSP**, is a framework for turning interaction behaviour into privacy-preserving synchronised signals.

RSP supports synchronisation without coercion, coordination without domination, minimal disclosure, consent-aware participation, visual feedback from weighted signals, expiry of unnecessary source data, and accountability without permanent surveillance.

## Core Flow

**Observe only what is necessary → translate behaviour into weighted signal → synchronise the visual state → act on the feedback → expire the signal → burn or decouple the identifiable source.**

## Identifiable Source Burn

**When user behaviour is translated, synchronised, aggregated, or converted into a protocol signal, any identifiable source information should be removed, destroyed, cryptographically erased, or irreversibly decoupled as soon as it is no longer necessary, unless retention is required by law, explicit consent, safety, or legitimate accountability.**

Short form:

**Translate behaviour. Synchronise the signal. Burn the identifiable source.**

## Signal Field Concept

RSP can be implemented as a **Signal Field**: a visual synchronous representation of engagement, friction, resonance, coordination health, or support need.

Raw events may include clicks, scroll depth, engaged time, active time on page, element interactions, form interactions, video pause/rewind/completion, quiz attempts, AI prompt retries, model handoffs, human corrections, escalation events, return visits, and conversion or completion events.

These are translated into low-resolution states such as dormant, active, resonant, friction, overload, cooling, converting, support needed, coordination healthy, and coordination degraded.

## Product Positioning

**RSP turns interaction behaviour into respectful, privacy-preserving signal fields for better coordination.**

Alternative:

**A privacy-preserving synchronisation layer for human, AI, and system feedback.**

## Non-Surveillance Boundary

RSP should not be used for hidden behavioural scoring, permanent clickstream profiling, emotional inference, employee surveillance, learner surveillance, or manipulative engagement loops.
