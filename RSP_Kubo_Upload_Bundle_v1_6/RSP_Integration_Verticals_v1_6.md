# RSP Integration Verticals

## 1. AI Model Congregations / Multi-Agent Systems

RSP can act as a feedback and coordination layer for groups of AI models, agents, tools, and human supervisors.

Signals: prompt volume, repeated prompts, model handoffs, tool-use loops, human corrections, escalations, safety events, successful completions, failed completions, and trust/override signals.

Product concept: **RSP AI Signal Layer** — a privacy-preserving coordination and observability layer for multi-agent AI systems.

## 2. LMS / Online Education

RSP can translate course interactions into respectful learning feedback.

Signals: lesson views, scroll depth, active time, video pause/rewind/completion, quiz attempts, wrong-answer patterns, assignment progress, discussion engagement, module drop-off, and return visits.

Product concept: **RSP Learning Signal Layer** — ethical learning analytics without permanent learner surveillance.

## 3. Product Analytics / UX

RSP can translate engagement into product resonance and friction maps using clicks, scrolls, feature adoption, forms, and conversion events.

## 4. Customer Support / AI Service Operations

RSP can show where support systems, AI bots, and human agents are synchronising or failing.

## 5. Workplace Collaboration

RSP can show project coordination health without employee surveillance.

## 6. Healthcare and Care Coordination

RSP can support care networks with minimal disclosure, consent, and strong retention controls.

## 7. Governance / DAOs / Civic Coordination

RSP can visualise proposal engagement, voting participation, consensus friction, and contributor energy.

## 8. Cybersecurity / Incident Response

RSP can represent alert pressure, response-team coordination, human overrides, and incident flow.

## 9. Creator Platforms / Communities

RSP can show community resonance without reducing participants to public scores.

## 10. E-commerce / Marketplaces

RSP can visualise product attention and buying friction without permanent individual shopper profiling.
