# RSP Genesis NFT — Tier Structure and Utility Model

**Protocol:** Respectful Synchronised Protocol (RSP)  
**Version:** v1.5  
**Creator:** Jack Oswald  
**Handle:** @johnoswald69  
**Date:** 2026-05-31  

## Positioning

RSP NFTs should be positioned as provenance, access, participation, certification, review, and licensing tokens — not as investment products.

The value of an RSP NFT should come from what it represents or unlocks within the RSP ecosystem, such as authorship provenance, founding participation, training access, implementation review, certification, or permission to use official RSP-aligned branding.

## Core Disclaimer

**RSP NFTs are utility, provenance, access, participation, and certification tokens. They are not investment products.**

No RSP NFT should promise profit, resale gain, yield, dividends, passive income, or guaranteed financial appreciation.

## Tier 0 — RSP Genesis NFT

**Supply:** 1  
**Purpose:** Origin, provenance, authorship, and symbolic protocol anchor.  
**Audience:** Founder, archival holder, institutional supporter, protocol patron.  
**Utility type:** Provenance.

### Utility

- Represents the original RSP Genesis declaration and artwork.
- Anchors the protocol version history.
- May be referenced as the symbolic root token of the RSP ecosystem.
- May include public acknowledgement as the Genesis holder, if transferred.
- May include one founder-level strategy session, if offered separately.

## Tier 1 — RSP Founder Pass

**Suggested supply:** 25–100  
**Purpose:** Early supporter access and protocol participation.  
**Audience:** Early contributors, advisors, community builders, privacy-first product founders.  
**Utility type:** Access and participation.

### Utility

- Access to early RSP development notes and drafts.
- Invitation to private founder briefings or calls.
- Ability to suggest protocol improvements.
- Early access to implementation templates.
- Recognition as an early RSP supporter.
- Optional allowlist for later certification or training tokens.

## Tier 2 — RSP Builder Pass

**Suggested supply:** 100–500  
**Purpose:** Access to implementation resources.  
**Audience:** Developers, designers, product teams, governance designers, privacy engineers.  
**Utility type:** Builder access.

### Utility

- Access to implementation guides.
- Access to RSP signal-design templates.
- Access to Identifiable Source Burn implementation checklist.
- Access to sample metadata, policy language, and consent-flow templates.
- Priority review queue for RSP-aligned product feedback.

## Tier 3 — RSP Certification Badge

**Supply:** Issued after review or training.  
**Purpose:** Verifiable credential for RSP-aligned people, organisations, or systems.  
**Audience:** Apps, organisations, practitioners, consultants, auditors, teams.  
**Utility type:** Credential.

### Utility

- Verifiable proof of RSP training, review, or alignment.
- May permit limited use of official “RSP-aligned” badge language if licensing terms are accepted.
- May reference the version of the protocol reviewed.
- May include expiry or renewal requirements.
- May be soulbound or non-transferable if used as a credential.

### Suggested certification categories

- RSP-Aware Practitioner
- RSP-Aligned Product
- Identifiable Source Burn Reviewed
- RSP Privacy-by-Design Review
- RSP Care/Coordination System Review

## Tier 4 — RSP Partner Licence NFT

**Suggested supply:** Limited and approval-based.  
**Purpose:** Commercial partnership and brand-use licence marker.  
**Audience:** Organisations, platforms, apps, training providers, implementation partners.  
**Utility type:** Licence marker.

### Utility

- Permission marker for approved use of RSP partner branding.
- Access to partner implementation materials.
- Annual or periodic review rights.
- Public listing as an RSP partner, if desired.
- May include agreed commercial terms outside the NFT.

The NFT should point to a separate written licence agreement. The NFT alone should not be relied upon as the full legal contract.

## Tier 5 — RSP Audit / Review Token

**Supply:** Service-based issuance.  
**Purpose:** Represent a completed review, audit, workshop, or implementation assessment.  
**Audience:** Product teams, communities, organisations, grant projects.  
**Utility type:** Review record.

### Utility

- Proof that a specific review or workshop was completed.
- Links to a review summary, if public.
- Records protocol version reviewed.
- Records scope of review.
- May include renewal date or expiry date.

## Suggested Collection Architecture

1. RSP Genesis — 1/1 provenance token.
2. RSP Access Passes — Founder and Builder passes.
3. RSP Credentials — certification badges, preferably non-transferable where appropriate.
4. RSP Partner Licences — approval-based partner markers.
5. RSP Reviews — audit and implementation review records.

## Recommended Starting Offer

Start with three simple assets:

1. RSP Genesis NFT — 1/1, symbolic and archival.
2. RSP Founder Pass — limited early supporter access.
3. RSP Builder Pass — implementation resources and review priority.

Add certification, partner licence, and audit/review NFTs later once RSP has real users, assessment criteria, and public demand.
