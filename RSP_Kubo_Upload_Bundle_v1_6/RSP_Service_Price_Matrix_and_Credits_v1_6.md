# RSP Service Price Matrix and Credit Layer

**Version:** v1.6  
**Currency assumption:** GBP  
**Credit rule:** 1 RSP Credit = £1 of redeemable RSP service value.

## Important Disclaimer

RSP Credits are prepaid service credits redeemable only for RSP services, resources, reviews, certification processes, and ecosystem access.

They are not cash, not investments, not redeemable for fiat, not designed for speculation, and do not represent equity, debt, yield, dividends, or profit share.

## Future Tier 6 — RSP Coordination Credit Layer

RSP Coordination Credits are future utility credits intended to account for access, contribution, review, certification, and implementation services inside the RSP ecosystem.

They are not designed as an investment product, profit-sharing instrument, stablecoin, e-money token, or general-purpose currency.
